What the columns mean
  • Symbol — Ticker (click to open its Yahoo Finance chart).
  • Date — Trading day.

  • Day vol — That day's total volume.

  • Vol vs avg — That swing vs. the stock's average daily range (2× = twice as volatile as usual).
